# Hi, I'm Sana

Android developer porting things to Valve's Steam Frame: VR games, streaming clients, the odd tool. Most of the newer ports run natively in the Frame's own SteamVR, with no Lepton, APK or PC needed.

## Projects

- [GalaxyFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/GalaxyFrame): Super Mario Galaxy in VR, native on the Frame.
- [PrimedFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/PrimedFrame): Metroid Prime in VR, native on the Frame.
- [OpenFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/OpenFrame): Halo: Combat Evolved in VR, native on the Frame.
- [LegendFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/LegendFrame): Tomb Raider: Legend in VR on the Frame.
- [KartFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/KartFrame): Mario Kart Wii VR on the Frame at 90 Hz.
- [AwakeningFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/AwakeningFrame): The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening as a VR tabletop diorama, native on the Frame. Pre-release.
- [Azahar for Frame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/AzaharFrame): the Azahar 3DS emulator with CitraVR's immersive VR modes, native on the Frame.
- [Moonlight XR for Frame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/moonlight-xr-steam-frame): stream games and your desktop from your PC to a virtual screen.
- [FramePassthrough](https://github.com/simplysanatori/FramePassthrough): passthrough shortcuts on the thumbsticks.

## How I work

These ports are AI-assisted. I start each project and do a fair amount of the coding, review and fixing by hand, all the way through. AI speeds up development, but its work is checked and reined in, with the focus on keeping every port as lightweight as possible.
