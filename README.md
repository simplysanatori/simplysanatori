# Hi, I'm Sana

Android developer porting things to Valve's Steam Frame: VR games, streaming clients, the odd tool. Most of the newer ports run natively in the Frame's own SteamVR, with no Lepton, APK or PC needed.

## Projects

- [GalaxyFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/GalaxyFrame): Super Mario Galaxy in VR, native on the Frame. Bring your own disc.
- [PrimedFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/PrimedFrame): Metroid Prime in VR, native on the Frame. Bring your own disc.
- [OpenFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/OpenFrame): Halo: Combat Evolved in VR, native on the Frame.
- [LegendFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/LegendFrame): Tomb Raider: Legend in VR on the Frame.
- [KartFrame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/KartFrame): Mario Kart Wii VR on the Frame at 90 Hz.
- [Moonlight XR for Frame](https://github.com/simplysanatori/moonlight-xr-steam-frame): stream games and your desktop from your PC to a virtual screen.
- [FramePassthrough](https://github.com/simplysanatori/FramePassthrough): passthrough shortcuts on the thumbsticks.

## How I work

These ports are AI-assisted. I pick the targets, direct the work, test on the headset and own the results. Each repo says so in its README.
